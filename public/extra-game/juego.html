Satellite mission, 1999–2024

From ice to anchovy

You watch the sea from space. Each round is a real year between 1999 and 2024. With only a few hours of satellite time, you have to guess how Peruvian fishing did.

  1. Look at signals. There are four from Antarctica and three from the coast of Peru. Each one costs 1 hour of satellite time, and you have 14 hours for 8 years.
  2. Decide. Did fishing go up or down compared to the year before? If you choose “certain” you win more, but you also lose more.
  3. Compare with what happened. You will see the real FAO data and which signals were right.
What do you want to predict?

With keyboard: 1–7 look at signals, S or B pick up or down, D or C the confidence and Enter confirms.
